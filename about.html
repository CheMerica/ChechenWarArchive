About the Archive

An independent project dedicated to documenting the human story of the Chechen conflicts.

Our Mission

The Chechen War Archive is an independent historical documentation project dedicated to preserving the record of the First and Second Chechen Wars — conflicts that reshaped the Caucasus and left an enduring mark on the people of Chechnya.

This archive exists to ensure that the names, faces, and stories of those who lived through these wars are not forgotten. Through the documentation of heroes, key locations, and major battles, the archive serves as a lasting reference for researchers, historians, students, and members of the Chechen diaspora worldwide.

The First Chechen War (1994–1996) and the Second Chechen War (1999–2009) together resulted in enormous loss of life and the near-total destruction of the Chechen capital, Grozny. Despite this devastation, the Chechen people demonstrated extraordinary resilience and a fierce determination to preserve their identity, culture, and sovereignty.

This project does not take a political stance. It is a record — an effort to document what happened, who was involved, where events unfolded, and why these conflicts matter to history. Contributions of photographs, testimonies, and corrections from researchers, veterans, and family members are always welcome.

The archive was created by Shamil Imakaev as a tribute to the courage and sacrifice of the Chechen people during one of the most intense and underreported conflicts of the late twentieth and early twenty-first centuries.

Documentation

The archive catalogs heroes, key battle sites, and geographic locations drawn from both the First and Second Chechen Wars, cross-referencing historical sources for accuracy.

Preservation

Many of the individuals and events documented here have received little coverage in Western media. This archive works to fill those gaps with reliable, sourced information.

Community

The archive welcomes contributions from historians, veterans, family members, and researchers. Corrections, photographs, and firsthand accounts are integral to keeping this record accurate.

Creator

Founded by Shamil Imakaev as an independent historical project. The archive is not affiliated with any government, political organization, or media outlet.
