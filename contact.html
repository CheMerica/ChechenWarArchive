Research Resources

External archives and tools available to researchers, families, and anyone tracing the history of these wars.

  1. External Archives
    • Qonahi Zama (Heroes Era) — one of the largest Chechen war media archives online, collecting photographs, video footage, and first-hand accounts from both wars. The name means "Heroes Era" in Chechen.
    • Available 24/7 via Telegram — free account required to view full media
    • Content: combat photography, portraits of fighters, personal testimonies, unit histories
  2. Identification Tools
    • Yandex Reverse Image Search — upload a photograph to find other instances of it across the web. Far more effective than Google for images from the post-Soviet world.
    • How to use: go to yandex.com/images, click the camera icon in the search bar, then upload or paste your image
    • Available 24/7, no account required — useful for identifying fighters, locations, and events in undated photographs
  3. Submit to This Archive
    • Accepts: photographs, biographies, battle accounts, corrections, and personal testimonies
    • Submissions reviewed personally — allow up to 7 days for a response
    • All contributions are credited to the source; anonymous submissions accepted on request
