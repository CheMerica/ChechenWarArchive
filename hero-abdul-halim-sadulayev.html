Abdul-Halim Sadulayev

1966 – 2006

Biography

Islamic scholar and third President of the Chechen Republic of Ichkeria, succeeding Aslan Maskhadov. Born in Argun, Chechnya, Sadulayev was a religious figure who assumed the presidency following Maskhadov's death in 2005.

As president, he attempted to consolidate the remaining armed resistance and maintain the legitimacy of the Ichkerian government in exile and in the field. He was known for combining his religious authority with political and military leadership during an extremely difficult period for the Chechen cause.

Sadulayev was killed by Russian security forces in his hometown of Argun in June 2006.
