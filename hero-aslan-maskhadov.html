Aslan Maskhadov

1951 – 2005

Biography

Military commander and politician, elected President of the Chechen Republic of Ichkeria. Born in the Shatoy District of Chechnya, Maskhadov rose through the ranks of the Soviet military before becoming one of the leading figures of the Chechen independence movement.

He led Chechen forces during the First Chechen War (1994–1996), negotiating the Khasavyurt Accords that ended the conflict and secured a period of de facto independence for Chechnya. Elected President in 1997, he struggled to maintain order during the interwar period.

When the Second Chechen War began in 1999, Maskhadov continued to lead the armed resistance. He was killed by Russian special forces during an operation in Tolstoy-Yurt in 2005.
