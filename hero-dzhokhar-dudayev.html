Dzhokhar Dudayev

1944 – 1996

Biography

First President of the Chechen Republic of Ichkeria and former Soviet Air Force general. Born in Yalkhori, Chechnya, Dudayev served in the Soviet military and attained the rank of Major General before returning to his homeland at the close of the Soviet era.

In 1990 he became chairman of the Chechen National Congress and in 1991 declared Chechen independence, triggering a prolonged confrontation with Moscow. He was elected President of the newly proclaimed Chechen Republic of Ichkeria and oversaw the early years of the independence struggle.

When Russia launched the First Chechen War in December 1994, Dudayev led the defense of Chechnya. He was killed on 21 April 1996 by a Russian missile strike that targeted his satellite phone signal.
