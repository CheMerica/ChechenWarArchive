Lechi Dudayev

1960 – 2000

Biography

Chechen resistance commander who fought throughout both Chechen wars. Born in the Shatoy District of Chechnya, Lechi Dudayev (unrelated to President Dzhokhar Dudayev) was an active participant in the armed struggle for Chechen independence from the earliest days of the conflict.

He served in the mountainous regions of southern Chechnya and was involved in operations throughout the First Chechen War and into the Second. His knowledge of the Shatoy terrain made him a valuable commander in that area.

Lechi Dudayev was killed in combat in 2000 during the Second Chechen War.
