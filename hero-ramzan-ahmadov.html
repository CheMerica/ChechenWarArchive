Ramzan Ahmadov

1970 – 2001

Biography

Chechen resistance commander who operated in the Urus-Martan district throughout both Chechen wars. Born in Urus-Martan, Chechnya, Ahmadov was a key figure in the local armed resistance against Russian federal forces.

He led operations in and around the Urus-Martan region during the First Chechen War and continued his resistance activities after the conflict resumed in 1999. Known among local fighters for his leadership in the district's defense.

Ahmadov was killed in combat in 2001 during the Second Chechen War.
