Ruslan Gelayev

1964 – 2004

Biography

One of the most prominent Chechen resistance field commanders, renowned for his military skill and determination. Born in Komsomolskoye, Chechnya, Gelayev became a central figure in the armed resistance during both Chechen wars.

He commanded forces in numerous major engagements and was known for his tactical boldness. The defense and eventual fall of his home village of Komsomolskoye in 2000 became one of the defining episodes of the Second Chechen War.

After the collapse of organized resistance, Gelayev continued guerrilla operations across the North Caucasus. He was killed in 2004 during an operation in the mountains of Dagestan.
