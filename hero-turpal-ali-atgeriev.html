Turpal-Ali Atgeriev

1969 – 2002

Biography

Deputy Minister of Internal Affairs of Ichkeria and one of the key resistance commanders. Born in Grozny, Chechnya, Atgeriev held significant responsibilities within the Chechen government and military structure.

He participated actively in the armed resistance during both Chechen wars, coordinating security and law enforcement operations on behalf of the Ichkerian authorities. His role made him a high-priority target for Russian forces.

Atgeriev was captured by Russian forces and died in Russian custody in 2002 under circumstances that have never been fully clarified.
