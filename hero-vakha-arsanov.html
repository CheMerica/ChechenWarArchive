Vakha Arsanov

—

Biography

Vice President of the Chechen Republic of Ichkeria under President Aslan Maskhadov. Vakha Arsanov was one of the key political figures of the Ichkerian government and played a significant role in the administration during the interwar period and the early years of the Second Chechen War.

As Vice President, he served alongside Maskhadov in attempting to govern and legitimize the Chechen Republic of Ichkeria on both domestic and international levels. His subsequent fate has not been reliably established in open sources.
