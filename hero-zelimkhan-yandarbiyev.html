Zelimkhan Yandarbiyev

1952 – 2004

Biography

Poet, political figure, and acting President of Ichkeria following Dudayev's death. Born in Staroy-Yurt, Chechnya, Yandarbiyev was a prominent Chechen writer and cultural activist before becoming deeply involved in the independence movement.

He served as Vice President under Dzhokhar Dudayev and assumed the role of acting President after Dudayev was killed in April 1996. He held the position until the presidential election later that year brought Aslan Maskhadov to power.

After the start of the Second Chechen War, Yandarbiyev lived in exile, continuing to advocate for Chechen independence. He was assassinated in a car bombing in Doha, Qatar, in February 2004.
